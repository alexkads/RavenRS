A to Z

    Components

    Every component reads the same design tokens, works with the keyboard and runs unchanged on desktop and in the browser. Open any of them for a live example, its code and its API.

    #Actions and inputs

    Buttons and controls that take a value.

    • Button - Clickable, focusable button with styles, sizes, icons, loading and selected states
    • IconButton - Square, icon-only button for toolbars
    • ToggleButton - Button that stays pressed, like bold or mute
    • ToggleGroup - Row of toggle buttons where one or several can be on
    • SegmentedControl - Row of mutually exclusive segments, like a compact tab bar
    • Toggle - On/off switch
    • Checkbox - Check box with a label
    • RadioGroup - One choice among several, shown as radio buttons
    • Slider - Draggable value between a minimum and a maximum, with a colored track or a neutral point
    • Stepper - Decrement, value and increment for small quantities
    • TextArea - Text on several lines, scrolling past its height or growing with the text
    • NumberField - A typed number or amount of money, formatted when you leave
    • MaskedField - Text that follows a pattern, like a phone number
    • TimePicker - A time of day, typed or picked from a list
    • DateTimePicker - A date and a time over one value
    • LookupField - Picks a record from a list searched on demand
    • FormLayout - Groups of fields in columns, tabs and a list of errors
    • FilterBuilder - Builds a filter out of lines, in the expression language
    • TagBox - Several values from a list, shown as tags
    • ColorPicker - A color from a palette, a shade square or typed
    • DateRangePicker - A period in two clicks, or ready-made
    • DropZone - Dashed area around any content, which takes dropped files
    • FileUpload - Files from the chooser or dropped, with a reason when refused
    • ImageField - A picture chosen by the person
    • Wizard - A task in steps that can refuse to be left
    • StepIndicator - Where a task in steps stands, for an app's own layout
    • StepBadge - The round mark of a step
    • Toolbar - Commands that fold into a menu when out of room
    • SplitButton - A main command with a menu of alternatives
    • ConfirmDialog - A question before something that cannot be undone
    • ColorSwatch - Round color to pick from a palette
    • ThemeSwitcher - Switches between Raven Dark, Raven Light and Raven Glass
    • ThemePicker - A small menu of themes that says which one is on.
    • ThemeEditor - Changes the tokens of the theme on screen, with live contrast and JSON to copy and paste.
    • FloatingToolbar - Translucent row of commands over its content, with a handle that moves it
    • ScrollEdgeArrow - A strip that scrolls, with an arrow at each end that has more to show
    • TitleBar - Title, actions and window buttons drawn by the app; does not move the window
    • ButtonGroup - Buttons glued side by side, as one control
    • ValueSlider - A value in small that opens a slider or can be typed
    • InlineRename - A name that turns into a field by a double click or F2
    • FavoriteToggle - Heart or star that marks a favorite: filled when on
    • SwatchGrid - Grid of color swatches; the handler gets the keys held with the click
    • NinePointAnchor - Three by three grid for a point of reference, moved by the arrow keys
    • ColorPair - Foreground and background colors, one over the other, with a swap button

    #Forms

    Fields, pickers and the layout around them.

    • TextField - Single-line text input with selection, clipboard, views inside and a clear button
    • Select - Picks one option from a list in a field-like trigger
    • Combobox - Select with a search box that filters the options
    • DatePicker - Field that opens a calendar in a popover
    • Calendar - Month grid to pick a date
    • EventCalendar - A month with its events inside each day
    • Chat - Conversation over a field to write in, with examples and cards to act on
    • RichText - Bold, italic, code and links in plain text, with the marks chat apps use
    • AttachmentStrip - Files about to go with a message, with size, how to send each one and a way to take it out
    • AudioPlayerButton - Plays and stops a recording, with progress and a transcript to open
    • SelectionBar - How many are chosen, a way out and what to do with them, with Escape to leave
    • FloatingPanel - Glowing sphere in a corner that opens a panel over the app and drags between corners
    • ModelPicker - Local AI models to download, try, use and remove
    • CodeBlock - A command to copy
    • ReleaseNotes - An app's versions and what each one brought
    • StatusBar - One fixed line at the foot of an app window
    • Timeline - Blocks along time on named tracks
    • ToolRail - A narrow bar of tools with their names under their icons
    • Scheduler - Timeline of resources: drag, resize and draw events, zoom the time axis
    • RecurrenceEditor - Edits how something repeats: how often, on which days, until when
    • InputOtp - Separate boxes for a one-time code
    • FormField - Labeled field with a hint and validation messages
    • Label - Form label with an optional required marker and hint

    #Navigation

    Moving around an application.

    • DocumentTabs - Tabs for open documents that close, move, rename and say what was not saved
    • FileBrowser - Folders and files from the app, with a breadcrumb, sorting and selection
    • YearView - The twelve months of a year, with marks on the days
    • Ribbon - Office-style command bar with tabs, groups and quick access
    • Menubar - Horizontal bar of menus such as File, Edit and View
    • Tabs - Tab bar with one content view per tab
    • Sidebar - Navigation column with sections, items and counts
    • SidebarBrand - The name of an app at the top of a sidebar
    • Breadcrumb - Path of locations ending at the current page
    • Pagination - Previous, numbered pages with ellipses, and Next
    • Accordion - Disclosures separated by hairlines
    • Disclosure - Header that shows or hides its content

    #Overlays

    Content that floats above the page.

    • Dialog - Modal window over a dimmed backdrop
    • Sheet - Panel that slides over the UI from one edge
    • SplashScreen - Opening screen that covers the window while the app loads
    • Popover - Floating content anchored below a view
    • HoverCard - Rich content shown while the pointer is over a trigger
    • Tooltip - Short label shown on hover, with an optional key cap
    • HelpTip - A small question mark that explains something
    • DropdownMenu - Button that opens a list of actions, with submenus and check marks
    • ContextMenu - Menu opened at the pointer on right click
    • CommandPalette - Searchable list of commands, opened with Cmd/Ctrl+K
    • Toaster - Stacked notifications that dismiss themselves

    #Data

    Showing records and numbers.

    • VirtualList - A list of any length with rows of any height, which jumps to a row and stays at the end
    • SortableList - Rows put in order by a drag or with Alt and the arrows
    • TreeView - Items inside items: opens, selects, checks and filters
    • CardView - Records as cards, only those in sight are built
    • DataGrid - Virtualized grid for 100,000 rows: sorting, filters, grouping and export
    • Table - Simple table with a header, hover rows and numeric columns
    • List - Bordered list with icons, titles and trailing text
    • Stat - Headline number with a label and a change indicator
    • Chart - Area, bar, line, pie, doughnut, radar and radial charts in the patterns of the shadcn/ui catalog, with tooltips, keyboard, a focus that glides between categories, lines of reference and a fixed axis; scatter, sparklines and candlesticks too
    • ChartReference - A dashed line at a value that matters, with a label, over a chart
    • SankeyChart - How much flows from what to what: ribbons between nodes, arrow keys, and a clear message when the flows go in a circle
    • ChartCard - A chart's card: title, description, what changes it, tabs with totals, and how it trends
    • BarChart - Bar chart with labels and faint gridlines
    • AreaChart - Smooth area chart of evenly spaced values
    • Barcode - QR Code and linear barcodes that scanners can read

    #Layout

    Arranging everything else.

    • TileLayout - A dashboard of tiles to drag and resize
    • VStack - Lays children out in a column
    • HStack - Lays children out in a row
    • Grid - Equal-width columns that wrap into rows
    • Spacer - Flexible space that pushes its siblings apart
    • ScrollView - Scrolling stack, vertical or horizontal
    • Splitter - Two panes with a draggable divider
    • SplitPanes - Any number of panes with a bar between each two, and limits
    • SplitHandle - A bar to drag that changes a size
    • DockArea - Panels in a tree of splits and tabs that move by a drag, by keys or by a menu, resize, collapse, close, and save their arrangement as text
    • Card - Grouped surface lifted off the canvas
    • GlassCard - Translucent card for Raven Glass compositions
    • Section - Titled group of content with an optional description
    • Divider - Hairline separator
    • Footer - Band that ends a page or app, with left, center and right areas
    • AspectRatio - Keeps content at a width-to-height ratio
    • Collapse - Animates its content's height as it opens and closes
    • ZStack - Views on top of each other, in a box as large as the largest
    • PropertyRow - A label on the left and its value on the right
    • PageHeader - Title, description, badges and actions at the top of a page

    #Display and feedback

    Text, status and progress.

    • Text - Text in the theme's type scale, with truncation
    • Alert - Tinted message box with an icon, a title and a description
    • Badge - Small pill for status, counts and categories
    • Avatar - Round avatar with initials colored from the name
    • Kbd - Keyboard key, for showing shortcuts
    • ProgressBar - Horizontal progress bar, with a label, a percentage and a detail
    • Spinner - Indeterminate activity indicator
    • Skeleton - Placeholder block shown while content loads
    • Dot - Small round mark of state, such as not saved
    • OverlayBadge - A small view on a corner of another
    • Rating - Stars for a score, to read or to give with the pointer and the keyboard
    • IconTile - An icon in a square tinted with its color
    • EmptyState - What an area shows when it has nothing: icon, title, sentence and actions
    • HeroBanner - Wide band in a strong color, readable from across the room
    • MultiProgress - Several progress bars stacked, for work with more than one part
    • ShimmerText - Text with a light passing over it, still when motion is reduced
    • DaySeparator - A line with a word in the middle that divides a list by day
    • ShortcutSheet - Dialog listing the keys of a screen, in groups
    • ShortcutHintBar - Line of key hints under what they act on

    #Media

    Pictures and custom drawing.

    • Filmstrip - A strip of thumbnails of any length, whose cells come from the app
    • PagedDocumentView - A bar with page and zoom over pages built only when they show
    • Icon - Vector icon from the built-in Lucide set
    • Image - Shows an image, with fit modes
    • Lottie - Plays Lottie animation files, drawn as curves on the GPU
    • Carousel - Slides with buttons, position dots and optional autoplay
    • Histogram - Overlapping channels from ready-made bins, linear or logarithmic, with clip marks
    • Ruler - Horizontal or vertical ruler in your units, with a round step for any zoom
    • ColorWheel - Hue and saturation on a disc, by a drag or the arrow keys
    • CurveEditor - Points you drag over a smooth curve, with an optional histogram behind
    • ZoomControl - Fit, 1:1, the percentage and buttons that zoom in and out
    • ZoomPanView - Zoom and pan with pinch, wheel, drag, keys and inertia, within limits
    • Minimap - Small copy of the content with a draggable rectangle around what is in view
    • RuleOfThirdsGrid - Composition lines over a picture: thirds, divisions, squares or the golden ratio
    • RubberBand - A rectangle drawn with a drag, in the units of the content, selected on release
    • Marquee - Marching ants around a rectangle, a path or loose segments
    • TransformHandles - Eight handles, a turn and a pivot over a box, returning its geometry
    • Protractor - A band of ticks along an arc that sets an angle
    • CropOverlay - A crop with a veil, proportion and angle, returning the rectangle and the angle
    • Eyedropper - An armed button that takes the color of the point clicked, supplied by the caller
    • CompareView - Two pictures side by side or across a divider you drag
    • ImageLightbox - A picture over the whole app, with zoom, arrows, Esc, caption and counter
    • Canvas - Box that draws whatever its closure appends to the scene

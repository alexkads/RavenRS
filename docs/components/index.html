Component parity with reference libraries

RavenRS aims to be more complete than the three reference libraries. This table tracks every component family they offer and where RavenRS stands. It is updated with each batch.

Legend: ✅ available · 🟡 partial (what is missing is noted) · ⬜ planned. Column S marks whether shadcn/ui ships it; D and T mark two commercial component suites for desktop and web.

Summary#

Families tracked ✅ 🟡 ⬜

Carousel / Scroll view ✓ ✓ ✓ ✅ Carousel

Empty state ✓ ✅ EmptyState, over IconTile

Day divider in a list ✅ DaySeparator
Typography scale ✓ ✓ ✓ ✅ Text styles
PDF viewer ✓ ✓ 🟡 PagedDocumentView: bar, zoom and virtual pages, with pictures from the app. Reading the PDF and searching it are up to the app
Filmstrip ✅ Filmstrip: virtual, cells from the app, selection with modifiers, keyboard, arrows on the ends
Histogram ✅ Histogram: channels from ready-made bins, added up as light, linear or logarithmic, with marks at clipped ends
Ruler ✅ Ruler: horizontal or vertical, in the units of the caller, with a round step for the zoom, origin and marker; vertical numbers stack, since text does not rotate
Color wheel ✅ ColorWheel: hue and saturation on a disc that is computed once and shared, with arrow keys and Shift to keep the hue
Curve editor ✅ CurveEditor: points over a monotone curve, added with a press, removed with a double click or Delete, moved with the arrows; baseline and histogram behind
Zoom control ✅ ZoomControl: fit, 1:1, percentage, + and −, through a ZoomView shared with ZoomPanView and Minimap
Zoom and pan ✅ ZoomPanView: pinch, Cmd/Ctrl with the wheel, wheel and drag to move, double click, keys, inertia that respects reduced motion, limits. Content is an image or a closure: the runtime has no transform for a subtree
Minimap ✅ Minimap: a small copy with the rectangle of what is in view, by the same ZoomView as ZoomPanView, with drag and arrow keys
Composition grid ✅ RuleOfThirdsGrid: thirds, equal divisions, squares or the golden ratio, in a box or over a ZoomPanView, taking no input
Rubber band selection ✅ RubberBand: a rectangle drawn with a drag in Binding, selected on release, with the keyboard; costs no builds while dragged if nobody else reads it
Selection outline ✅ Marquee: marching ants around a rectangle, a path or loose segments, which stop and ask for no frames with reduced motion
Transform box ✅ TransformHandles: eight handles, turn and pivot over a BoxTransform in content units, with Shift and Alt as in image editors and the keyboard. It returns geometry and turns nothing, since the renderer has no transforms; CursorIcon has no diagonals, so corners show the hand
Protractor ✅ Protractor: a band of ticks along an arc that sets an angle by a drag from anywhere, a double click back to zero, keys and value
Crop ✅ CropOverlay: on TransformHandles and the lines of RuleOfThirdsGrid, with a veil outside, fixed proportion and angle, returning rectangle and angle. The frame turns over a still picture, since the renderer has no transforms
Eyedropper ✅ Eyedropper and EyedropperArea: an armed button that takes the color of the point clicked, which the caller supplies with a closure or EyedropperState::pick; Esc disarms. It does not capture the screen, and CursorIcon has no dropper, so the cursor is the hand
Compare images ✅ CompareView: two images side by side or with a divider dragged or moved with the arrows, through ImageQuad.uv, with captions and an optional chosen side
Image lightbox ✅ ImageLightbox: a picture over the whole app with zoom by ZoomPanView, arrows, Esc, caption and counter, from Labels
Image editor ✓ ⬜
Text with marks and links ✅ RichText: *bold*, _italic_, ~struck~, `code`, fenced blocks, lists, quotes and web addresses as links with click and keyboard, wrapped word by word. ravenrs-text has one style per text and no italic or monospace font, so italics are told apart by a muted color and code by a chip
Attachments of a message ✅ AttachmentStrip: icon or thumbnail, name that gives way, size, a menu for how to send it when there is a choice and a button to remove it; the app holds the list
Audio player ✅ AudioPlayerButton: play and stop over SoundProvider::speak_clip, progress by the clock, drawn without rebuilding, audio the app can deliver after the press, and a transcript to open or ask for. A new recording sends the old one back to rest. It has no seek, since the service does not report or change where it is
Bar over a multiple selection ✅ SelectionBar: the count from Labels, a way out by button or Esc, and actions over Toolbar, which go to a menu when they do not fit and wait for something chosen
Chat ✓ ✓ ✅ Chat, with marks and links by RichText and a photo, a document or a recording in the bubble by ChatMedia; MovieChat over training films
Choosing local AI models ✅ ModelPicker; ravenrs_ai::ModelManager offers only what fits

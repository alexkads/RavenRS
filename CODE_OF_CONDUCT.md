# Code of conduct

RavenRS follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome newcomers, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and discrimination are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer through the email on the [GitHub profile of @alexkads](https://github.com/alexkads). Reports are handled confidentially.

Crate flow_whiteboard 

Source
Expand description

A diagramming whiteboard, made only with FlowCanvas, FlowToolbox, FlowInspector and a SegmentedControl: a flowchart with step lines and bend points, and a mind map that arranges itself as a tree; both grow from the keyboard (Cmd/Ctrl+Enter) and export.

It validates the component as a whiteboard of this kind: everything here is the library’s, and what it could not do is written in the README.

Expand description

A flow: its nodes and connections, and the revision they are at.

Fields§

§format: u32

The version of the JSON it was read from or will be written as.

§id: String

Unique among the app’s flows.

§name: String

What the person called it.

§revision: u32

Which revision it is; the app raises it on each saved change.

§nodes: Vec<NodeDef>

The nodes, in the order they were made.

§connections: Vec<Connection>

The connections, in the order they were made.

impl FlowDefinition

Source

pub fn to_graph(&self, catalog: &Catalog) -> FlowGraph

The flow drawn for a FlowCanvas: a node card per node, with the ports of its kind, titled by its title or its kind’s, shown as disabled when it is, and the node’s whole setup in its data; one connection per connection; the flow’s id, name and revision in the graph’s data. The nodes are laid out in columns by how far they are from a start, which the app then moves as the person draws.

Source

pub fn from_graph(graph: &FlowGraph, catalog: &Catalog) -> FlowDefinition

The flow as the person left the drawing: its id, name and revision from the graph’s data, its nodes and connections the graph’s, in the graph’s order. Each node takes its setup from its card’s data, so a pasted card brings the setup of the node it was copied from under its new id; a card with none (drawn from the toolbox) starts with no parameters. A title the person typed over the kind’s is kept, and the disabled look sets disabled. Groups are drawing only, and are left out.

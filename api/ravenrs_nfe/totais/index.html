ravenrs_nfe

Module totais 

Source
Expand description

The totals of a document, always calculated and never typed.

§Rule adopted

  • vProd of a line is qCom * vUnCom, rounded to 2 places with half away from zero.
  • The totals vProd and vDesc are the sum of the lines, so they never round again.
  • vNF is vProd - vDesc: freight, insurance, others, ST, IPI and II do not exist in this POC.
  • vTroco is what was paid above vNF. Paying less is refused, except with tPag=90.
  • A value with more places than the layout allows is refused, never rounded in silence.

§Sources

MOC 7.0, Anexo I: rules I11-10 (with a tolerance of R$ 0.01 and “rounded to two places”), W07-10, W10-10, W16-10 (tolerance of R$ 0.50), YA03-10 and YA09-10. The MOC does not say how the midpoint rounds; half away from zero is a choice here. This crate writes exact values, so it needs no tolerance; the sandbox applies them, as the rules say.

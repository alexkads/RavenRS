The homologation addresses of the POC. There are no production addresses in the crate.

§Origin

The web service addresses were read from the table storage/wsnfe_4.00_mod55.xml and wsnfe_4.00_mod65.xml of sped-nfe, in November 2026. They are facts about public servers and were written here by hand, for a few states only.

§Not checked

  • The national environment comes from the entry AN of the same table.
  • None of them was called: that needs an A1 certificate and an accredited taxpayer.
  • The official list of states served by SVRS was not read. SC, RJ, ES and DF go to SVRS because the two tables give them no address of their own.
  • The QR Code addresses come from the same table. url_consulta_chave has no table at all: its values are from memory, and a wrong one gets rejection 878 from the real SEFAZ. Use EmissorBuilder::url_consulta to replace it. See REGRAS.md.

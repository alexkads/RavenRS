Crate ravenrs_widgets_imaging 

Source
Expand description

RavenRS instruments for pictures, fed by the caller’s data: Histogram, Ruler, ColorWheel, CurveEditor, ZoomPanView, Minimap, CropOverlay, Eyedropper, CompareView, ImageLightbox and more, and Lottie (feature lottie).

Applications depend on ravenrs-widgets (or ravenrs), which re-exports all of this by the same names.

Structs§

BoxTransform
A rectangle turned around a point, which is what a TransformHandles edits.
ColorWheel
Picks hue and saturation on a disc, by a drag or the arrow keys: left and right turn the hue, up and down change the saturation, with Shift for bigger steps. Holding Shift during a drag keeps the hue. A double click or Home takes the color out, keeping the hue.
CompareView
Two pictures to tell apart: side by side, or one over the other with a divider between what shows of each. The divider follows a drag from anywhere, the arrows (with Shift, further), Home and End, and a double click puts it in the middle. A picture that does not have the proportions of the box is fitted in it, centered.
CropArea
What a CropOverlay edits: a rectangle of the content and how far it is turned.
CropOverlay
A crop over a picture: what is out of the frame goes dark, with thirds inside the frame and a fine grid while it turns. It is built on TransformHandles and draws the same lines as RuleOfThirdsGrid.
CurveEditor
Editable points over a smooth curve that never swings past them. A press on empty space adds a point and a drag moves it; a double click or Delete removes one, except the two ends. From the keyboard, the arrows move the chosen point (with Shift, further), Page Up and Page Down choose another, and Enter adds one on the curve halfway to the next.
Eyedropper
The button that arms an eyedropper, which looks pressed until a color is picked or Escape disarms it. The color comes from an EyedropperArea or from EyedropperState::pick: nothing here can read the screen.
EyedropperArea
Where an eyedropper picks from, over a picture. While armed, a click asks sampler for the color at that point of the content, in the units of the space; a point out of the content or a None picks nothing and leaves it armed. While not armed it takes no input.
EyedropperState
What an Eyedropper and an EyedropperArea share, and how a color reaches them: from the area, or from whoever knows the color under the pointer through pick. Nothing here can read the screen, so the color always comes from the caller.
Histogram
Overlapping channels from ready-made bins, in a linear or logarithmic scale, with a mark at each end when too much of a channel piles up there. Where channels overlap, their colors add up, as light does, so each stays readable.
HistogramChannel
One series of a Histogram: ready-made bins and the color that shows them.
HueSaturation
A color as a ColorWheel holds it, without brightness.
ImageLightbox
Pictures over the whole app, one at a time, with zoom by ZoomPanView, a caption and a counter. It shows while open holds the index of a picture, and Escape or its X set it to None.
LightboxItem
One picture of an ImageLightbox.
Lottie
Plays a Lottie animation, drawn with curves on the GPU, sharp at any size. It sleeps when it ends or is paused, so that an idle window stays idle. Where motion is reduced it shows its last frame, still.
Marquee
The animated outline of a selection, the “marching ants”: a dark line with light dashes walking over it, which reads over any picture. Give it a rectangle, a closed path or loose segments, such as the edges of a mask, in the units of the space.
Minimap
A small copy of the content with a rectangle around what is in view, linked to a ZoomPanView by the same ZoomView. A press or a drag moves the view there, and so do the arrow keys (with Shift, further).
Protractor
A band of ticks along an arc that sets an angle, by a drag from anywhere on it. A double click goes back to zero. The arrows change it by a step (with Shift, ten), Home and End go to the ends, and Delete goes back to zero.
RubberBand
A rectangle drawn with a drag, in the units of the content. The selection follows the drag through its binding, and on_select gets it on release; a press and release without moving clears it. Escape cancels a drag.
RuleOfThirdsGrid
A composition grid over a picture: thirds by default, or the other CompositionGuides. It takes no input, so what is underneath still gets the pointer.
Ruler
A horizontal or vertical ruler in the caller’s units. It picks a round step for the scale, so marks never crowd as the zoom changes. Numbers on a vertical ruler stack digit over digit, since text does not rotate.
TransformHandles
Eight handles around a box, one that turns it and the point it turns around, drawn over content in the units of a ContentSpace. It edits a BoxTransform and draws nothing but the box: the renderer cannot turn a picture, so applying the geometry is up to whoever owns it.
ZoomPanPaint
Where drawn content goes, for ZoomPanView::custom and Minimap::custom.
ZoomPanView
Zoom and pan over content: pinch or Cmd/Ctrl with the wheel to zoom, a drag or the wheel to move, a double click between fit and 100%, and the keys +, -, 0, 1 and the arrows (with Shift, further). A thrown drag coasts to a stop, which asks for frames only meanwhile and never happens with reduced motion. The content cannot leave the view.

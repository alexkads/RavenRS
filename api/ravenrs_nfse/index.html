BETA, proof of concept. Not homologated. Production is blocked.

NFS-e of the national standard (Padrão Nacional): the service provider writes a DPS, signs it, and the Sefin Nacional answers with the NFS-e. It is not issued through the city halls any more, and not through the SEFAZ: the authority is the CGNFS-e, with the Receita Federal. See docs/NFSE.md for the sources, and what is still unconfirmed.

The smallest proof of concept:

  • provider ME/EPP of the Simples Nacional (opSimpNac=3, regApTribSN=1), tpAmb=2 only;
  • one service per DPS, ISS taxable and not withheld, the taker optional;
  • POST /nfse, HEAD and GET /dps/{id}, GET /nfse/{chave}, the cancellation e101101 and the agreement of the city;
  • recovery after a lost answer, by the same DPS Id: the part that matters most at a counter.

Everything leaves through [ravenrs_dfe::Transport]; sandbox::SefinSandbox intercepts it the way MSW does, and checks what the Sefin would check.

ⓘ
let producao = ravenrs_nfse::Ambiente::Producao;
use ravenrs_nfse::sandbox::SefinSandbox;
use ravenrs_nfse::{Ambiente, BetaAck, Emissor, exemplo};

let emissor = Emissor::builder(BetaAck::i_understand_this_is_experimental())
    .ambiente(Ambiente::Sandbox)
    .signer(ravenrs_dfe::certificados::certificado_curinga()?)
    .transport(SefinSandbox::new())
    .build()?;
let emitida = emissor.emitir(&exemplo::dps(1))?;
assert_eq!(emitida.chave.as_str().len(), 50);

Modules§

chave
The access key of the NFS-e: 50 digits, given by the Sefin in its answer.
danfse
What the DANFSe has to have, from the NT 008 (v1.02, 14/07/2026): since 03/08/2026 the API of the DANFSe is suspended and the issuing software draws it. One page, portrait, at least A4, a QR Code of at least 1.52 cm, and “NFS-e SEM VALIDADE JURÍDICA” when tpAmb=2. The layout itself is a report of the standard designer (ravenrs-app-nfe), which anyone can change.
dps
The XML of the DPS: deterministic, with no spaces or line breaks, in the order of the XSD v1.01.
evento
The request for an event: here only the cancellation, e101101.
exemplo
A sample DPS, for tests, documentation and the sandbox. The CNPJs are the usual test ones.
modelo
The DPS of the proof of concept: one service, ISS taxable and not withheld, provider ME/EPP.
retorno
Reading the answers of the Sefin: JSON around documents in GZip + Base64.
sandbox
A sandbox of the Sefin Nacional, in the style of MSW: it implements [Transport], so the emitter builds the same bytes and calls the same addresses, and nothing leaves the computer.

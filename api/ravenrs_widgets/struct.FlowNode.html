Expand description

A box of the diagram, in content units.

Fields§

§id: String

Unique within its graph.

§kind: String

What sort of node it is, a name of the app’s: it picks the icon, the caption and the color the node is drawn with.

§title: String

What it is called.

§subtitle: Option<String>

A line under the title.

§x: f32

Left edge.

§y: f32

Top edge.

§width: f32

Width.

§height: f32

Height.

§ports: Vec<FlowPort>

Where connections start and end, spread evenly along each side in this order.

§status: FlowNodeStatus

How it is doing.

§parent: Option<String>

The group it sits in, by id. Its place stays its own (in units of the diagram, like any node’s), and it moves when the group moves.

§group: bool

A group: a box that holds the nodes whose parent it is, drawn behind them with its title on top.

§rotation: f32

Turned around its middle by this many degrees, clockwise on screen. Its ports turn with it; x, y, width and height stay those of the unturned box.

§shape: FlowShape

The outline it is drawn in.

§style: FlowStyle

Its colors and the place of its title, apart from the theme.

§hidden: bool

Left out of the drawing for now, with its connections, as a layer turned off; it stays in the diagram and in its files.

§data: Value

What the app keeps for the node: its setup, the kind of thing it stands for. The diagram carries it without reading it: it is copied, pasted, undone and written with the node. When it names other nodes, the paths to those names listed under NODE_REFS are renamed when the node is pasted with new ids.

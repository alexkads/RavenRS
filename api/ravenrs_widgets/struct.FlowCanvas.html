Expand description

A diagram of nodes joined by connections, on an endless surface with a dotted grid.

  • Drag the surface or turn the wheel to pan; pinch, or turn the wheel with Cmd/Ctrl, to zoom around the pointer; +, - and 0 zoom in, out and to the whole diagram.
  • Press a node to select it (Shift adds to the selection), drag it to move every selected node, and Shift-drag the surface to select with a rectangle. Arrows move what is selected one grid step (with Shift, four), Delete takes it out and Escape lets it go.
  • Drag from a port to another to connect them. While dragging, the ports that would accept the connection light up; one that refuses turns red, and the reason shows by the pointer. With the keys, Tab to a port, press Enter, Tab to the other port and press Enter again.
  • Select a connection and drag one of its ends, which show as handles, to another port to move that end.
  • Drag the corner of a selected node, or press Alt with the arrows, to resize it. The arrange button in the controls lays the diagram out in layers (FlowLayout).
  • Cmd/Ctrl+G puts the selected nodes in a group, which moves, copies and goes with what it holds; Shift+Cmd/Ctrl+G takes the selected groups apart. Dropping a node inside a group puts it there, and dropping it outside takes it out.
  • Double-click a node, or press F2 (or Enter on a selected node), to type its title in place: Enter keeps it, Escape gives it up.
  • Drag the round handle above a selected node to turn it, 15 degrees at a time (with Shift, freely), or press Alt+Shift with the arrows; Alt+Shift+0 puts it straight. Groups do not turn.
  • With the pointer over a node, arrows show on its sides: a click on one makes the next node there, joined to it, with its title open to type. Cmd/Ctrl+Enter does it to the right of the selected node, Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Enter below (FlowCanvas::next_node).
  • Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes, Shift+Cmd/Ctrl+Z (or Cmd/Ctrl+Y) redoes. Cmd/Ctrl+C copies the selected nodes with the connections between them, as JSON, Cmd/Ctrl+X cuts them and Cmd/Ctrl+V pastes them back with new ids, a little further along each time.

The diagram is the app’s, through a Binding: every move, connection and deletion is written back to it. Nodes say what they are with a FlowKind registered by name.

use ravenrs_reactive::State;
use ravenrs_widgets::{
    FlowCanvas, FlowEdge, FlowEndpoint, FlowGraph, FlowKind, FlowNode, FlowPort, IconName,
};

let graph = State::new(
    FlowGraph::new()
        .node(FlowNode::new("sale", "trigger", "New sale", 0.0, 0.0).port(FlowPort::output("out")))
        .node(FlowNode::new("stock", "action", "Check stock", 280.0, 0.0).port(FlowPort::input("in")))
        .edge(FlowEdge::new("e1", FlowEndpoint::new("sale", "out"), FlowEndpoint::new("stock", "in"))),
);
let canvas = FlowCanvas::new(graph.binding())
    .kind("trigger", FlowKind::new(IconName::Zap, "Trigger"))
    .kind("action", FlowKind::new(IconName::Package, "Action"));

pub fn connection( self, shape: impl Fn(&FlowGraph, FlowEdge) -> FlowEdge + 'static, ) -> FlowCanvas

Shapes every connection made or moved on the canvas: shape gets the diagram and the plain connection (its id and ends set) and gives it back with its kind, look and caps, as a notation draws that kind of line between those ends.

pub fn on_export( self, export: impl Fn(FlowExportFormat, FlowPicture) + 'static, ) -> FlowCanvas

Offers Export in the controls, and Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+E: the diagram, or with “Only the selection” what is selected, goes to export as a picture in the format chosen, PNG, SVG or PDF (on paper colors), which ravenrs_flow_export::saver turns into a file handed to the person. Exporting changes nothing, and works when locked too.

pub fn arrivals(self, arrivals: Binding<Option<FlowNode>>) -> FlowCanvas

Takes the nodes left here, as a FlowToolbox given the same binding leaves the one picked with Enter: each is copied to the middle of the view, with an id of its own, selected, and the binding emptied.

pub fn history(self, history: Binding<FlowHistory>) -> FlowCanvas

Shares what can be undone and redone, to offer undo and redo buttons elsewhere (with FlowHistory::can_undo) or to forget it when another diagram is opened. Without it, the canvas keeps its own. Undo puts back the whole diagram as it was, so a change the app made to the diagram in between goes too.

pub fn checks( self, checks: impl Fn(&FlowGraph) -> Vec<FlowIssue> + 'static, ) -> FlowCanvas

Offers Check in the controls: what checks finds wrong shows in a box over the canvas, kept up to date while it is open, and the nodes it is about are outlined.

pub fn runs(self, rules: FlowRunRules) -> FlowCanvas

Offers Debug in the controls: the diagram runs a step at a time as rules say, tokens going from the starts along the connections. The nodes they are at, have left and stopped at wrongly show it, the ways taken move, a way out is chosen where there is a choice, F9 puts a breakpoint on the nodes selected, and what happened is listed.

Takes from notation how its kinds look, which connections may be made and how they are drawn, what comes next from the keys, and what makes a diagram right.

Decides which connections may be made, beyond the rules that always hold: an output goes to an input of another node, once.

pub fn on_change( self, guard: impl Fn(&FlowGraph, &FlowGraph, &FlowChange) -> Result<(), String> + 'static, ) -> FlowCanvas

Asked before every change the canvas makes to the diagram, with the diagram before and after and what the change is; an Err refuses it and says why. A drag is asked once, when it is let go, and a refused one puts the nodes back.

use ravenrs_reactive::State;
use ravenrs_widgets::{FlowCanvas, FlowChange, FlowGraph};

let graph = State::new(FlowGraph::new());
let canvas = FlowCanvas::new(graph.binding()).on_change(|_, _, change| match change {
    FlowChange::Delete { .. } => Err("Published steps stay".into()),
    _ => Ok(()),
});

pub fn content( self, content: impl Fn(&FlowNode) -> Option<Box<dyn View>> + 'static, ) -> FlowCanvas

Puts any view inside the nodes it is given for: content is asked for each node (not groups) on every build, and a node it answers shows that view inside its card, laid out at the node’s size in units of the diagram and drawn at the zoom, its text sharp at any zoom. The view’s own controls take the pointer and the keys; where it has none, the node is pressed, dragged and connected as ever. A node without a view shows its icon and words.

use ravenrs_reactive::State;
use ravenrs_widgets::{AnyView, FlowCanvas, FlowGraph, Text};

let graph = State::new(FlowGraph::new());
let canvas = FlowCanvas::new(graph.binding()).content(|node| {
    (node.kind == "note").then(|| Box::new(Text::new(node.title.clone())) as AnyView)
});

pub fn open_end( self, models: impl IntoIterator<Item = (impl Into<String>, FlowNode)>, ) -> FlowCanvas

Letting go of a connection where no port is, or pressing Enter again on the port it started from, opens a search of these models there, each with the words it is found by. Only the models the port accepts are listed, by the same rules as any connection, and choosing one creates it there, connected, as one change: one Undo takes both away. Escape closes the search and creates nothing.

Source

pub fn execution(self, run: FlowRunView) -> FlowCanvas

Shows a run that happened outside the canvas, as the app tells it: each node’s status, drawn and said to screen readers, and on each connection how many items went along it, after its own words. Only the drawing changes, never the graph; give the run again as it moves on, and a node it does not name keeps its own status.

Source

pub fn cables(self, cables: bool) -> FlowCanvas

Draws the connections as cables that hang, swing and settle: a new one drops into its sag, moving a node or an end makes its cables swing, and one taken away falls. Off by default. Under reduced motion they show at rest; exports draw them at rest.

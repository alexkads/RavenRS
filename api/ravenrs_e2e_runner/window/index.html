Module window

Expand description

Running with the app’s real window on screen (--headed, #729): the mode, the pace a person can follow and the window kept open to look at a failure.

A run with no window is the default, for build servers and agents. With the window, the scenario, its blocks, their waits and their codes are the same; the app is opened with --e2e-serve --e2e-window, shows its window on its test data, and each step lands on the interface the window shows (ravenrs_e2e_wire::Headed). On top of that:

  • slow-mo waits a moment after each step, in the tool, while the app is idle and its window keeps drawing. It is not a step and has no timeout, so it never makes a step fail; the app’s clock keeps running, as it does for a person.
  • Control pauses before the next block, runs exactly one more, or stops the run; the window keeps drawing all along, since the app only waits for its next command.
  • KeepOpen leaves the window to the person at the end, to look at how the app was left; the run waits until they close it, and no process is left behind.

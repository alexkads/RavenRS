Panels of jacks joined by cables (#578). PatchPanel: a panel of jacks where cables are plugged and unplugged with the pointer or the keys, as on the back of an amplifier, a rack of effects, a modular synthesizer or an old telephone switchboard (#578).

ⓘ
let links = cx.use_state(Vec::<PatchLink>::new);
cx.child(
    &PatchPanel::new(
        vec![
            Jack::output("out", "Saída L").signal("áudio").at(40.0, 40.0),
            Jack::input("in", "Entrada 1").signal("áudio").at(40.0, 200.0),
        ],
        links.binding(),
    )
    .on_connect(|link| println!("{} → {}", link.from, link.to)),
);

The cables are CablePlugs: they hang, swing and settle, the one in the hand becomes the plugged one without a jump, and the panel asks for frames only while some cable moves. A cable goes from an output to an input of the same signal, one to an input; an output takes many. Dragging the plug of a plugged input takes it out to plug it elsewhere, and letting it go on the panel’s empty space unplugs it. With the keys, a jack picks up a cable with Space, the arrows go to another jack and Enter plugs it there; Delete unplugs the jack’s cables and Ctrl+Z undoes.

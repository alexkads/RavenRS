Crate ravenrs_app_nfe 

Source
Expand description

BETA, proof of concept. A module that issues NF-e and NFC-e from a RavenRS application. It is not homologated with any SEFAZ and cannot issue in production: everyone using an application with it sees a notice that cannot be dismissed, and nothing it issues has fiscal value.

The rules of the document are in ravenrs-nfe, which knows nothing about applications. This crate keeps what was issued as records, runs the sending out of the way of the screens and makes the receipt a report people can change in the standard designer.

The NFS-e of the national standard comes through ravenrs-nfse on the same service: the DPS is kept before it goes, and one that never got a sure answer goes again as it is.

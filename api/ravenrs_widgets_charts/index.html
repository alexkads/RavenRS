Crate ravenrs_widgets_charts 

Source
Expand description

RavenRS charts and diagrams: Chart and its kinds, Barcode, SankeyChart (feature charts) and FlowCanvas (feature flow), drawn with ravenrs-graphics like the reports draw them.

Applications depend on ravenrs-widgets (or ravenrs), which re-exports all of this by the same names.

Structs§

AreaChart
A smooth area chart (sparkline style) of evenly spaced values.
BarChart
A bar chart with labels under each bar and faint gridlines.
Barcode
A QR Code or a linear barcode, drawn so that every module lands on whole pixels. Scanners need dark on light, so it stays that way in dark themes.
Chart
A chart of any ChartKind. Its shapes are drawn into a picture at the screen’s density, and its labels are real text. Over the picture it says what each value is, under the pointer or with the arrow keys. ChartKind::Scatter and the sparklines stay as plain pictures, which take no focus, until given a tooltip.

FlowLayers
The layers of a diagram: its nodes from the front one down, what a frame holds under it and set in. A row selects its node on the canvas sharing the selection; its buttons hide and show the node (with what it holds and its lines), and bring it forward or send it back, each a change the shared history undoes.
FlowLayout
How FlowLayout::apply arranges a diagram: the kind of arrangement, the direction and the room between layers and between the nodes of a layer, in units of the diagram.

FlowNode

FlowNodeRun

FlowNotation
A notation for diagrams, such as a process notation or a flowchart’s: the elements it has, grouped for a palette, how each kind looks and is called, how connections between them are drawn, which ones may be made, what comes next from the keys, what makes a diagram right and the files it is kept in.
FlowPicture

FlowRunRules

FlowRunView

FlowSelection

FlowShelf
Every document a library keeps, in the order they were made.
FlowStyle
What a node shows apart from the theme. Every part left out is the theme’s.
FlowText
One run of words of a FlowPicture, on one line.
FlowToolbox
The models of node for a FlowCanvas, in groups that open and close, with a field that keeps only those whose words match. Each tile shows the model’s shape and its words.
FlowViewport
Which part of the diagram is in view: where its origin lands in the view and how large it is drawn. FlowCanvas writes it as people pan and zoom, and anything sharing the binding can move the view.
SankeyChart
How much flows from what to what. Each flow names its two ends, and nodes are created as they are named. Ribbons go from the color of their source to that of their target; under the pointer or with the arrow keys, what is connected stands out and the tooltip says how much. A screen reader hears every flow.

Constants§

FLOW_FORMAT
The version of the JSON FlowGraph::to_json writes. Version 1 had no format field; FlowGraph::from_json reads it and every version up to this one.
FLOW_NODE_DRAG
The kind of a DragPayload that carries a FlowNode to a FlowCanvas, as a FlowToolbox does: dropped on the canvas, a copy of the node is made there, with an id of its own.
NODE_REFS
The field of a node’s data that lists, as JSON pointers (/target, /routes/0/to), where its data names other nodes of the diagram by id. Pasting the node renames those ids as the pasted nodes are renamed.

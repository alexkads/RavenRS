RavenRS DockArea, its arrangement (dock::layout), geometry (dock::geometry) and drawing (dock::area), and the Ribbon.

Applications depend on ravenrs-widgets (or ravenrs), which re-exports all of this by the same names.

Structs§

DockArea
Panels in a tree of splits and groups of tabs, at the left, the right, the top, the bottom and the center of a space. Bars between them resize with the pointer or the keyboard, docks collapse from the strips on their edges, and panels close and come back.
DockChild
A part of a split and how much of it it takes.
DockLayout
The arrangement of a DockArea: which panel is where, the sizes, and what is collapsed, hidden at an edge, floating or closed. It holds ids only, so it can be kept in a State, compared and saved; what a panel shows comes from the app, by id, each time it is drawn.
DockPanel
A panel of a DockArea: what it is called and whether it can close. What it shows comes from the function given to the area, by its id.
Ribbon
The ribbon.
RibbonGroup
A labeled set of commands inside a page.
RibbonItem
A command in a ribbon group: a button, a toggle, or a button with a menu.
RibbonPage
A tab of the ribbon.

Enums§

DockChange
What a person is about to change in a DockArea, as its on_change is told. Panels are named by id.
DockCloseAction
What closing a panel of a DockArea does.
DockDrop
Where a panel is sent by DockLayout::move_panel. Places are named by a panel that is there, since the id of a panel is what stays the same as the tree changes around it.
DockEdge
A side of a group of tabs, where a split opens.
DockLayoutError
Why a saved text could not be read. Nothing was changed; the app goes on with the arrangement it has, or the one it starts from.
DockNode
A part of the tree: tabs, or parts side by side or stacked.
DockPanelKind
What a panel of a DockArea is: a document goes in the center, a tool in the docks around it, once the area has documents (see DockPanel::kind).
DockTabBar
When a group of a DockArea shows its tabs.
RibbonSize
How much room a ribbon command takes.

Type Aliases§

DockGuard
Decides whether a person may make a change, given the arrangement before and after it. When they may not, the arrangement stays as it was, nothing is saved, and the reason shows.

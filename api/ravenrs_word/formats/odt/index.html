OpenDocument Text (.odt, ODF 1.3).

Both ways, through the flow model (crate::formats::model) with what ODT keeps besides (model::FlowOpts): paragraphs and headings with their spacing, indents, line spacing, contextual spacing, alignment, borders, background and tab stops; character formatting (the extras in model::Fmt::extra included); custom paragraph and character styles by name, and the look of the heading, title, quotation and preformatted styles; lists with their identity, number formats, labels and positions, and heading numbering (text:outline-style); tables with column widths, spans, header rows, cell borders, padding, shading and vertical alignment; pictures (in line or floating), equations as formula objects, text boxes; links, bookmarks, text fields, footnotes and endnotes, comments with ranges and replies; sections with their own master pages, page setup, columns and headers and footers; document defaults, metadata and LibreOffice’s layout settings (settings.xml, kept from the file or Word-compatible).

Export writes mimetype (stored, first), META-INF/manifest.xml, content.xml, styles.xml, meta.xml, settings.xml, pictures under Pictures/ and formula sub-documents. Import reads content.xml, styles.xml (style inheritance resolved, automatic and common styles), meta.xml, settings.xml and the parts frames point at; drawing shapes are still skipped.

Tracked changes (ODF 1.3 Part 3 §5.5) go both ways: text:tracked-changes holds one text:changed-region per change; an insertion is the body text between text:change-start/text:change-end, a deletion keeps the deleted paragraphs in its region and leaves a text:change point in the body. They map to Document::revisions and the ins/del marks of runs and paragraph marks; a deleted paragraph mark is a region of several paragraphs. Format changes are read as no change.

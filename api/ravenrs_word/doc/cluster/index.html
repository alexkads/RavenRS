Module cluster 

Source
Expand description

Text clusters for complex scripts (Myanmar, Khmer, Tai Tham, the Indic scripts): where the caret may stop, what Backspace removes, and where Myanmar text may wrap.

Extended grapheme clusters (UAX #29) split some syllables that a shaper draws as one unit: a Myanmar consonant stacked under a virama (မ္ဘ), the Myanmar vowel signs U+102B/U+102C and the visarga U+1038 (excluded from the grapheme SpacingMark class), Khmer subscript consonants after the coeng. A caret between those would sit inside a glyph cluster, so the caret steps and layout clusters both use joins_cluster on top of the grapheme rules.

joins_cluster
No caret stop between prev and next although a grapheme boundary falls there: next is a sign that belongs to the syllable before it, or prev stacks the next consonant.
myanmar_breaks
Line-break opportunities inside Myanmar text, which has no spaces between words: before each syllable (UAX #14 leaves class SA to a dictionary; Word breaks Myanmar between syllables). A consonant starts a syllable unless it is stacked (after U+1039) or a final (followed, perhaps after a medial, by the asat U+103A, the virama U+1039 or the dot below U+1037). Byte offsets, sorted; only between two Myanmar characters.
next_cluster
The next cluster boundary after off (text.len() at the end).
prev_cluster
The previous cluster boundary before off (0 at the start).

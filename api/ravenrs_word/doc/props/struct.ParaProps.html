Paragraph formatting. None = inherit.

Fields§

§style: Option<String>

Paragraph style id.

§align: Option<Align>§indent_left: Option<f32>

Left indent, points.

§indent_right: Option<f32>§indent_first: Option<f32>

First-line indent, points; negative = hanging.

§indent_left_chars: Option<f32>

Left (start) indent in characters (w:leftChars / w:startChars, stored by Word in hundredths). A non-zero value wins over indent_left; one character is the size of the paragraph mark’s font. Some(0.0) cancels an inherited value. Common in East Asian documents.

§indent_right_chars: Option<f32>

Right (end) indent in characters (w:rightChars / w:endChars), like indent_left_chars.

§indent_first_chars: Option<f32>

First-line indent in characters (w:firstLineChars; negative = w:hangingChars), like indent_left_chars.

§space_before: Option<f32>§space_after: Option<f32>§space_before_auto: Option<bool>

HTML-style automatic space before (w:beforeAutospacing): 14 pt, none at the top of the document or of a table cell, and collapsing with the space after the paragraph above.

§space_after_auto: Option<bool>

HTML-style automatic space after (w:afterAutospacing): 14 pt, none at the end of a cell.

§line_spacing: Option<LineSpacing>§contextual_spacing: Option<bool>

Ignore space before/after between paragraphs of the same style.

§mirror_indents: Option<bool>

w:mirrorIndents (ECMA-376 §17.3.1.18): the left and right indents are the inside and outside ones, so they swap sides on even (left-hand) pages.

§adjust_right_indent: Option<bool>

w:adjustRightInd: automatically adjust the right indent for the document grid when enabled; keep the specified right indent when disabled.

§keep_next: Option<bool>§keep_lines: Option<bool>§page_break_before: Option<bool>§widow_control: Option<bool>§outline_level: Option<u8>

0..=8 = Level 1..9; 9 = body text.

§numbering: Option<NumRef>§tabs: Option<Vec<TabStop>>

Tab stops (replaces the inherited list; TabAlign::Clear stops remove inherited ones).

§shading: Option<Rgb>§borders: Option<Borders>§suppress_hyphens: Option<bool>§suppress_line_numbers: Option<bool>§bidi: Option<bool>§text_direction: Option<ParagraphTextDirection>

Paragraph text direction (w:textDirection); None inherits from the paragraph style.

§drop_cap: Option<u8>

Drop cap: lines to drop (0 = none) — applies to the first character(s).

§drop_cap_margin: Option<bool>

Drop cap “In margin” (w:dropCap="margin"): the letter hangs in the left margin and the text keeps its indent. Unset = dropped into the text.

§drop_cap_distance: Option<f32>

The drop cap’s distance from the text, in points (w:hSpace). Unset = 0.

§drop_cap_font: Option<String>

The drop cap letter’s font family (the cap run’s w:rFonts). Unset = the text’s font.

§frame: Option<TextFrame>

A text frame (w:framePr other than a drop cap): the paragraph is placed apart from the text, which may wrap around it — a footer’s page number in a frame, for instance.

§kinsoku: Option<bool>

Asian typography (w:kinsoku): East Asian line-breaking rules — no line starts with closing punctuation such as 、。」 or ends with opening punctuation such as 「（. Unset = on.

§word_wrap: Option<bool>

w:wordWrap: Latin words wrap whole; false lets them break at any character (Word’s “Allow Latin text to wrap in the middle of a word”). Unset = on.

§overflow_punct: Option<bool>

w:overflowPunct: punctuation may hang past the line end. Unset = on.

§top_line_punct: Option<bool>

w:topLinePunct: compress punctuation at the start of a line. Unset = off.

§auto_space_de: Option<bool>

w:autoSpaceDE: automatic space between Asian and Latin text. Unset = on.

§auto_space_dn: Option<bool>

w:autoSpaceDN: automatic space between Asian text and numbers. Unset = on.

§num_change: Option<Box<NumChange>>

Tracked change of the list numbering (w:numberingChange).

§fmt_change: Option<Box<PropChange<ParaProps>>>

Tracked formatting change (w:pPrChange): the paragraph properties before it.

§snap_to_grid: Option<bool>

w:snapToGrid: lines snap to the section’s document grid. Unset = on.

§text_alignment: Option<TextAlignment>

w:textAlignment: how characters of different sizes line up on a line. Kept for the file (not laid out yet); Word sizes a line by it, so dropping it changes Word’s pages.

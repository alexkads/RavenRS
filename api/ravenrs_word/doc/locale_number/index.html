Module locale_number

Numbers written the way a language writes them: 1 250,50 in Polish, 1.250,50 in German, 1,250.50 in English. Used by table sort and table formulas.

Functions§

decimal_separator
The decimal separator of language lang (a BCP 47 tag; None means English).
format_number
Write v the way language lang writes a formula result: whole numbers without decimals, others with two decimals and the language’s decimal separator (5,75 in Polish).
parse_number
Parse s as a number written in language lang, or None when it isn’t one. The decimal separator is the language’s; group separators are spaces (and . or ' where the decimal separator is a comma, , or ' where it is a point) between groups of three digits. A currency sign or short currency word is ignored, and so is a trailing % (12 % is 12, as table sort reads it). In comma languages a lone . that cannot be a group separator (1.5) is read as a decimal point. A trailing separator with no digits after it (1., an ordinal in a numbered column) is the whole number. A minus may be -, − or an en dash.

MAX_TABLE_DEPTH
Most tables nested one inside another. Pasting and inserting stop there, so no edit builds a document deep enough to overflow the stack when it is saved, copied or dropped (#1596), or deeper than a .json save reads back: serde_json reads 127 nested containers, a table level takes six, and the deepest cell of a header, note or text box needs up to 15 more for its paragraph and the richest content measured (#3618). The DOCX reader and writer keep 24.

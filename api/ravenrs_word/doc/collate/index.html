Module collate 

Source
Expand description

Language-aware sorting of text: a small, pure-Rust collation.

Every letter gets a primary weight (its place in the alphabet) and a secondary one (its diacritic). Strings compare by primary weights first, then by secondary ones, so é sorts with e (resume < résumé < rex) unless the language makes it a letter of its own: Polish puts ą right after a and ż after ź after z, Swedish puts å ä ö after z, Czech puts ch after h, Spanish ñ after n, Hungarian cs, gy, sz… after their first letter. The per-language tables follow the published alphabets of those languages. Text without diacritics sorts exactly as a case-insensitive code-point compare.

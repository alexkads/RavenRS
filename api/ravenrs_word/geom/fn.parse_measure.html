pub fn parse_measure(s: &str, default: Unit) -> Option<f32>
Expand description

Parse a measurement typed by a user: 1", 1 in, 2.5cm, 12pt, 3 pi, 10 mm, 1.5 li (lines are 12 pt). A bare number is in default. Returns points. Either decimal separator works (2,5 cm and 2.5 cm), whatever the region.

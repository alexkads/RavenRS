RavenRS time on screen: the Scheduler and its time axis, the EventCalendar with its RecurrenceEditor, and the Timeline.

Applications depend on ravenrs-widgets (or ravenrs), which re-exports all of this by the same names.

Structs§

CalendarEvent
Something on a EventCalendar’s day.
EventCalendar
A month with its events inside each day, for agendas and bookings. Use a Calendar to pick a date and this to see what is on them.
RecurrenceEditor
Edits a RecurrenceRule: how often, every how many, on which weekdays and until when, and says in words what it chose. start is the first day, which the rule counts from.
Resource
A row of a Scheduler: a person, a room, a machine, an account.
Scheduler
A timeline of resources: a row for each person, room or account, a time axis across the top, and events as bars that can be dragged along the time and between rows, resized by their ends, and drawn on an empty stretch. Weekends are shaded, and only what is in view gets built, so large plans stay smooth.
SchedulerChange
An event moved, resized, or drawn for the first time: where it would now be.
SchedulerEvent
A bar on a Scheduler. One with no length shows as a milestone.
SchedulerRange
A shaded stretch of time across all rows, such as a holiday or a deadline.
TimeAxis
The stretch of time a Scheduler shows, how wide it is and how it is marked.
TimePoint
A moment on a calendar, to the minute, with no time zone.
Timeline
Things along time, on named tracks, under a ruler with markers and a playhead.
TimelineItem
One block on a TimelineTrack.
TimelineMarker
A named moment, on the ruler of a Timeline.
TimelineTrack
A row of a Timeline.

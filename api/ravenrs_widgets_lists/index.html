RavenRS long lists and what is built on them: VirtualList, Filmstrip, PagedDocumentView, FileBrowser, YearView, SortableList, DocumentTabs, TreeView, and DataInspector.

Applications depend on ravenrs-widgets (or ravenrs), which re-exports all of this by the same names.

Structs§

DataInspector
Looks at JSON items as a table, a tree, the JSON itself or their schema.
DayMark
What marks a day of a YearView.
DocumentTab
An open document in a DocumentTabs.
DocumentTabs
Tabs for open documents, as a browser or an editor has: they close, change places by dragging or with Alt and the arrows, are renamed with a double click, F2 or the menu, carry a color and a dot for what was not saved, and scroll with arrows when they do not fit. The arrows alone move between them, and Delete closes. The tabs belong to the app, which applies what the handlers tell it.
FileBrowser
An explorer of folders and files: a breadcrumb that goes back, a header that sorts, and a list of any length with icon, name, size and date. The entries are data from the app, which answers on_navigate and on_open with another folder; it never touches the file system, so it serves a backup on a server as well as a disk. A click selects alone, Ctrl or Cmd adds, and Shift takes a range. The arrows, Home and End move, Space adds, Enter opens, and Backspace goes up.
FileEntry
A folder or file of a FileBrowser. Nothing here touches the file system: it says what the app knows, wherever that came from.
FileSort
The order of a FileBrowser. Folders always come first.
Filmstrip
A horizontal strip of thumbnails that builds only what shows, so thousands cost the same as a few. Each cell comes from whoever uses it, with its own image, stars or badges; the strip draws the frame around it. A click selects alone, Ctrl or Cmd adds, and Shift takes a range; the arrows, Home and End move and keep the selected cell in view, Space adds, Enter activates. Arrows appear on the ends that have more to show.
FilmstripCell
What a cell of a Filmstrip is told when it is drawn.
PagedDocumentView
A bar with the page, the total and the zoom, over a column of pages of which only those that show are built, so the pictures of the others need not exist. Pictures come from whoever uses it, page by page: until one is ready, the page shows a placeholder of the right size. The fit follows the width of the view. Page Up, Page Down, Home and End move between pages, and +, - and 0 zoom.
SortableList
A list that reorders by dragging a row, with a line showing where it will land, or with Alt and the arrows, Home and End. The arrows alone move between rows, and each move is announced to screen readers. The order belongs to the app, which applies what on_reorder tells it.
TreeNode
One item of a TreeView, with the items under it.
TreeView
A list of items that open to show more items: a chooser, a menu of a module, the structure of a document. Arrows move and open, Enter opens the item, Space checks it or selects it. With a height, only the visible lines are built.
VirtualList
A list of any length that builds only the rows that show, with rows of any height. They are laid out and measured as they appear, and what was measured is kept by key, so going back finds them at the right size. It can jump to a row, stay at the end as a chat does, and keep what is on screen still when rows come in above it. With nothing to select, the arrows, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End scroll; with a selection or on_activate, they move between rows, and Enter or Space activates.
YearView
The twelve months of a year, with a dot on the days that have something and a click on any of them. As many months go on a line as fit. From the keyboard the year is one Tab stop: arrows move by day and week, Home and End to the ends of the week, Page Up and Page Down by month, and Enter or Space picks.

Module outline 

Source
Expand description

Outline groups (Data ▸ Group) of a sheet’s rows or columns: the runs of lines at or above each outline level, as the grid draws them (brackets, − / + buttons, level buttons) and the Show / Hide Detail commands act on them. Summary rows sit below their detail and summary columns right of it, or above / left of it when the sheet’s outline settings say so (Sheet::summary_below, Sheet::summary_right).

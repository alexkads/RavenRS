Perspective (projective) transforms between quadrilaterals: what straightening a photographed page needs. Pure maths in f64; every input may be hostile, so degenerate or non-finite quadrilaterals give None instead of NaNs.

order_quad
The four corners in reading order for a y-down space (view or raster pixels): top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left, whatever order they were given in. None unless they form a convex quadrilateral with a non-negligible area.

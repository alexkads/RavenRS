Expand description

A JBIG2Decode stream’s data, with what its /JBIG2Globals stream decodes to, decoded as the filter gives it: rows rows of columns 1-bit samples, most significant bit first, each starting on a new byte, with black as 0 (JBIG2 codes black as 1).

The JBIG2 page has to be the image’s size, columns × rows: PdfCraft’s renderer draws the whole page into the image, so the rows of a page of another height wouldn’t be the image’s. The decoder decodes the page whole, so it is checked before it is decoded, and has to fit in max bytes as such rows and have at most MAX_JBIG2_PIXELS. Regions inside it are bounded by the decoder alone: a region may claim more pixels than its page, and is decoded before it is cut to the page. With no rows asked for, nothing is decoded.

Errors: data or globals that don’t decode, or a page of another size (FilterError::Corrupt); a page past max (FilterError::LimitExceeded) or MAX_JBIG2_PIXELS (FilterError::Unsupported, or Corrupt from the decoder, which has the same cap and can refuse the page first).

Decoding spends from work: what decoding may still cost across a run of images, in the decoder’s count of steps. There is no way in without it, so a run of images can’t decode them without sharing an allowance. Reading data and globals is charged first ([crate::third_party::hayro_jbig2::charge_reading]), so an image that turns out not to decode has paid for that, then what decoding takes ([crate::third_party::hayro_jbig2::Image::decode_within]). An image takes at most what one decode may on top of its reading and its page, whatever work allows, and one that work can’t pay for spends all of it. With nothing left an image isn’t read at all, so the images after that one cost nothing but this check, however large their globals.

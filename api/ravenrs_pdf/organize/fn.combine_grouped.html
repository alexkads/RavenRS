pub fn combine_grouped(runs: &[Run<'_>]) -> Result<Document, OrganizeError>
Expand description

Combine Files where a file’s pages may be split into several runs with other files’ pages between them. Each file (group) is copied once, all its pages together, then the pages are put in the runs’ order: its links between its own pages and its form fields stay whole across its runs, its attachments come along when its runs use all of its pages, and it gets one top-level bookmark (the title of its first run) at its first page, its own bookmarks nested. Otherwise as combine_selected (print standard, shared resources).

Struct ImageBudget 

Source 
pub struct ImageBudget { /* private fields */ }
Expand description

What one operation over a document’s images may still spend decoding them: exporting them, optimizing them, and storing LZW streams with Flate as it does, clearing them for redaction.

An image has its own limit, which doesn’t bound a file of thousands of images made to reach it: a few hundred bytes of JBIG2 can ask for seconds of decoding, 4 KB of Flate inside Flate for a gigabyte, and an image that shares /JBIG2Globals decodes them again. So images share this allowance, whatever they do with what they decode and whether or not it works, and past it they aren’t decoded. A caller then treats them as it does an image it can’t read, and ImageBudget::refused says how many there were.

An image also pays for its pixels once decoded, as what reads it goes through them one by one however few bytes coded them: blank rows of fax or Flate take almost none. It pays for no more than its data holds, a bit a pixel at least, so an image declaring a size its data doesn’t fill costs what a smaller one would, not the run’s allowance. A JPEG pays for its samples when it is decoded (ImageBudget::pay_jpeg), and is otherwise not charged, as JPEG 2000 isn’t: their decoders don’t run here.

pub fn decode( &mut self, doc: &Document, s: &Stream, max: usize, ) -> Result<Vec<u8>, CosError>

The samples of image s, decoded and charged to the allowance: the one way to decode an image outside the renderer, so that an operation over many images can’t decode them without one.

They go through its filters (Stream::decoded), or, for a CCITT fax or JBIG2 image (its last filter), through the filters before it and then crate::filters::decode_ccitt_within with its Document::fax_params, or crate::filters::decode_jbig2_within with its /JBIG2Globals, as CCITTFaxDecode and JBIG2Decode give them (ISO 32000-2 §7.4.6, §7.4.7). Parameters given by reference count, for every filter (Document::with_direct_parms). A fax image’s /Columns has to be its /Width, and a JBIG2 image’s page its /Width × /Height: PdfCraft’s renderer draws the whole page into the image. Up to its /Height rows are decoded, whatever a fax image’s /Rows says; damaged fax data gives fewer, so a caller checks it has the rows it needs. Damaged JBIG2 data, and globals that can’t be read (within MAX_JBIG2_GLOBALS), are an error.

No stage, the filters before a fax or JBIG2 decoder included, may produce more than max bytes, nor more than MAX_DECODED. A JBIG2 image’s /JBIG2Globals are another stream, shared between images, and are decoded within MAX_JBIG2_GLOBALS whatever max is. Each filter as it runs, the globals when they have to be read, and the fax or JBIG2 decoder spend from the allowance, and the image then pays for its pixels; with nothing left an image is refused before anything is read, its globals included.

let samples = ravenrs_pdf::cos::ImageBudget::new(&doc).decode(&doc, &s, 1 << 20);
assert_eq!(samples.unwrap(), b"");

Document has no way of its own to decode an image:

ⓘ
let _ = doc.decoded_image(&s);
ⓘ
let _ = doc.decoded_image_within(&s, 1 << 20);

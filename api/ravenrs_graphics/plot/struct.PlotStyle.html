Expand description

The look of a chart. Start from PlotStyle::new and change what differs.

Fields (Non-exhaustive)§

This struct is marked as non-exhaustive
Non-exhaustive structs could have additional fields added in future. Therefore, non-exhaustive structs cannot be constructed in external crates using the traditional Struct { .. } syntax; cannot be matched against without a wildcard ..; and struct update syntax will not work.
§kind: ChartKind

The kind of chart.

§colors: Vec<Color>

Series colors, or slice and ring colors, repeated as needed.

§item_colors: Vec<Color>

Colors per category, over the colors of the series.

§ink: Color

Text that matters, such as the number in the center.

§muted: Color

Text around the data.

§grid: Color

Gridlines.

§surface: Color

What is behind the chart. Slices are kept apart by a real gap, so it no longer paints anything.

§track: Color

The track behind a ring.

§size: f32

The font size of the text around the data.

§curve: Curve

How lines go from point to point.

§stack: Stack

How series share a category.

§gradient: bool

Fills areas from strong at the top to faint at the bottom, and bars from strong at the tip to fainter at the base.

§fill_opacity: f32

How solid an area or a radar polygon is.

§horizontal: bool

Bars lie down, with their categories on the left.

§reversed: bool

The value axis runs the other way: bars hang from the top, or if they lie down they grow to the left from the right.

§min_bar: f32

The shortest a bar with a value gets, however small the value is.

§labels_at_zero: bool

The categories of bars sit on the line of zero, on its other side from the bar, instead of along the edge of the chart.

§bar_radius: f32

The corners of a bar.

§max_bar: f32

The widest a bar gets.

§negative: Option<Color>

The color of a bar below zero.

§rising: Color

The color of a candle that closed at or above where it opened.

§falling: Color

The color of a candle that closed below where it opened.

§active: Option<usize>

The category standing out: a slice comes forward, a bar gets an outline.

§dots: bool

Draws a dot at each point of a line or a radar.

§value_labels: Option<LabelAt>

Where the values are written, if at all.

§category_labels: Option<LabelAt>

Where the categories are written beside the data, if at all.

§format: Rc<dyn Fn(f64) -> String>

How numbers are written.

§tick_format: Rc<dyn Fn(&str) -> String>

How categories are written on the axis.

§grid_lines: Grid

Gridlines of a chart with axes.

§category_axis: bool

Writes the categories along the axis.

§value_axis: bool

Writes numbers along the axis.

§tight_scale: bool

The scale fits the values instead of starting from zero.

§separator: f32

The gap between slices; zero for none.

§slice_radii: Vec<f32>

How far each slice reaches, in order, as a share of the room from the hole to the edge. One with no number here reaches the edge.

§label_guides: bool

Labels outside a round chart sit in a column on each side, none over another, each joined to its slice by a line with an elbow.

§hole: f32

The hole of a doughnut or of rings, as a share of the radius.

§concentric: bool

One ring per series, instead of only the first series.

§polar_grid: PolarGrid

The grid of a radar or radial chart.

§polar_fill: bool

Fills the grid of a radar chart with the first color.

§spokes: bool

Lines from the center of a radar chart to each category.

§rings: usize

How many rings the grid of a radar chart has.

§lines_only: bool

Radar polygons with no fill.

§radius_axis: bool

Writes numbers along the radius of a radar chart.

§start_angle: f32

Where rings start, in degrees clockwise from the top.

§sweep: f32

How many degrees the largest ring covers.

§tracks: bool

Draws the track behind each ring.

§center: Option<(String, String)>

A big number and a caption in the middle of a doughnut or of rings.

§rules_apart: bool

Leaves gridlines out of Plot::layers and says where they go in Plot::rules, for a screen that can line them up with its pixels and fade the data without them. Pages leave this off and get everything in the layers.

§bars_apart: bool

Leaves bars out of Plot::layers and says where they go in Plot::bars, for a screen that draws them itself at no cost when the size changes. The gridlines they cross are left out from behind them, so that a bar that fades shows no line through it.

§grid_dashed: bool

Gridlines in dashes.

§value_ticks: usize

About how many steps the value axis has; round numbers decide how many exactly.

§grid_columns: Option<usize>

Upright gridlines evenly spread into this many columns, instead of at each category or number, and whatever Self::grid_lines says of the others.

§ends_aligned: bool

The first category along the axis starts at its point and the last one ends at its point, so that they line up with the ends of a line or an area.

§references: Vec<Reference>

Lines across the data at values that matter, such as a target.

§domain: Option<(f64, f64)>

The value axis goes exactly from the first number to the second, and what is past them is cut off, instead of the axis fitting the values.

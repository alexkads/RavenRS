What a Connect 2.0 charge.paid webhook gives the fiscal document.

The webhook has no CNPJ of the acquirer: it comes from the table, with an alert. The documentation’s table says metadata.schemeName and its example says metadata.scheme_name; the example is what is read, and the other is accepted too.

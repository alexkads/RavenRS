1//! **BETA, proof of concept.** A module that issues NF-e and NFC-e from a RavenRS application. It is
2//! not homologated with any SEFAZ and cannot issue in production: everyone using an application with
3//! it sees a notice that cannot be dismissed, and nothing it issues has fiscal value.
4//!
5//! The rules of the document are in `ravenrs-nfe`, which knows nothing about applications. This
6//! crate keeps what was issued as records, runs the sending out of the way of the screens and makes
7//! the receipt a report people can change in the standard designer.
8//!
9//! The NFS-e of the national standard comes through `ravenrs-nfse` on the same service: the DPS is
10//! kept before it goes, and one that never got a sure answer goes again as it is.
11

1//! OpenDocument Text (`.odt`, ODF 1.3).
2//!
3//! Both ways, through the flow model ([`crate::formats::model`]) with what ODT keeps besides
4//! ([`model::FlowOpts`]): paragraphs and headings with their spacing, indents, line spacing,
5//! contextual spacing, alignment, borders, background and tab stops; character formatting (the
6//! extras in [`model::Fmt::extra`] included); custom paragraph and character styles by name, and
7//! the look of the heading, title, quotation and preformatted styles; lists with their identity,
8//! number formats, labels and positions, and heading numbering (`text:outline-style`); tables with
9//! column widths, spans, header rows, cell borders, padding, shading and vertical alignment;
10//! pictures (in line or floating), equations as formula objects, text boxes; links, bookmarks,
11//! text fields, footnotes and endnotes, comments with ranges and replies; sections with their own
12//! master pages, page setup, columns and headers and footers; document defaults, metadata and
13//! LibreOffice's layout settings (`settings.xml`, kept from the file or Word-compatible).
14//!
15//! Export writes `mimetype` (stored, first), `META-INF/manifest.xml`, `content.xml`,
16//! `styles.xml`, `meta.xml`, `settings.xml`, pictures under `Pictures/` and formula
17//! sub-documents. Import reads `content.xml`, `styles.xml` (style inheritance resolved, automatic
18//! and common styles), `meta.xml`, `settings.xml` and the parts frames point at; drawing shapes
19//! are still skipped.
20//!
21//! Tracked changes (ODF 1.3 Part 3 §5.5) go both ways: `text:tracked-changes` holds one
22//! `text:changed-region` per change; an insertion is the body text between
23//! `text:change-start`/`text:change-end`, a deletion keeps the deleted paragraphs in its region
24//! and leaves a `text:change` point in the body. They map to `Document::revisions` and the
25//! `ins`/`del` marks of runs and paragraph marks; a deleted paragraph mark is a region of
26//! several paragraphs. Format changes are read as no change.

2//! Clean-room pure-Rust video codecs for the Craft apps, one feature per codec. Input and output
3//! are byte slices and planar sample buffers (no filesystem access of its own); builds for
4//! `wasm32-unknown-unknown`.
5//!
6//! | Feature | Module | Codec |
7//! |---|---|---|
8//! | `dnx` | [`dnx`] | Avid DNxHD / DNxHR (SMPTE ST 2019-1): decoder and encoder |
9//! | `apv` | [`apv`] | APV (RFC 9924): decoder (`apv-decode`) and encoder (`apv-encode`) |
10//! | `vp8` | [`vp8`] | VP8 (RFC 6386): decoder (`vp8-decode`) and key-frame encoder (`vp8-encode`) |
11//! | `mpeg2` | [`mpeg2`] | MPEG-2 video (ITU-T H.262) and MPEG-1 video: decoder |
12//! | `mpeg4` | [`mpeg4`] | MPEG-4 Part 2 video (ISO/IEC 14496-2, Xvid/DivX): Simple profile encoder (`mpeg4-encode`) |
13//! | `prores` | [`prores`] | Apple ProRes (SMPTE RDD 36): decoder and encoder |
14//! | `h264` | [`h264`] | ITU-T H.264 / AVC: decoder (`h264-decode`) and encoder (`h264-encode`) |
15//! | `hevc` | [`hevc`] | ITU-T H.265 / HEVC: decoder (`hevc-decode`) and encoder (`hevc-encode`) |
16//! | `vp9` | [`vp9`] | VP9: decoder (`vp9-decode`) and encoder (`vp9-encode`) |
17//! | `av1` | [`av1`] | AV1 (AOMedia Video 1): decoder (`av1-decode`) and encoder (`av1-encode`) |
18//!
19//! No codec is on by default. Feature `threads` spreads the work of the codecs that support it
20//! over rayon worker threads.
21//!
22//! The decoders bound the pictures they hold with a memory budget ([`budget`]).
23//!
24//! Containers and bit-level I/O are in `craft-media`; the codecs here share its `bitstream`
25//! module and describe their streams with `crate::craft::media::CodecParams`.

2//! Media containers and bit-level I/O for the Craft apps. Codecs are in other crates
3//! (`craft-audio`; video later), which depend on this one.
4//!
5//! - `bitstream` (feature `bitstream`): MSB-first bit reader and writer, Exp-Golomb codes,
6//!   emulation-prevention escaping and NAL unit splitting.
7//! - `isobmff` (feature `isobmff`, enables `bitstream`): a clean-room ISO BMFF (MP4) and
8//!   QuickTime (MOV) demuxer and muxer, and a box reader for other ISO BMFF formats.
9//! - `matroska` (feature `matroska`): a clean-room Matroska (MKV) and WebM demuxer and muxer.
10//! - `ogg` (feature `ogg`): a clean-room Ogg demuxer with Ogg Opus and Ogg Vorbis stream mapping.
11//! - `avi` (feature `avi`): a clean-room AVI (RIFF) and OpenDML demuxer.
12//! - `mxf` (feature `mxf`, enables `chrono`): a clean-room MXF demuxer and writer (OP1a, OP-Atom).
13//! - `mpegts` (feature `mpegts`): a clean-room MPEG-2 Systems demuxer (transport streams and
14//!   program streams).
15//! - [`Container`] and [`CodecId`] (no feature): the identifiers the codec crates report a file's
16//!   container and codec with.
17//!
18//! `bitstream` and `isobmff` are on by default; `matroska`, `ogg`, `avi`, `mxf` and `mpegts` are opt-in. A crate that needs
19//! only bit I/O uses `default-features = false, features = ["bitstream"]`.

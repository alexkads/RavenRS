1//! **BETA, proof of concept. Not homologated. Production is blocked.**
2//!
3//! NFS-e of the national standard (Padrão Nacional): the service provider writes a DPS, signs it,
4//! and the Sefin Nacional answers with the NFS-e. It is not issued through the city halls any more,
5//! and not through the SEFAZ: the authority is the CGNFS-e, with the Receita Federal. See
6//! `docs/NFSE.md` for the sources, and what is still unconfirmed.
7//!
8//! The smallest proof of concept:
9//! - provider ME/EPP of the Simples Nacional (`opSimpNac=3`, `regApTribSN=1`), `tpAmb=2` only;
10//! - one service per DPS, ISS taxable and not withheld, the taker optional;
11//! - `POST /nfse`, `HEAD` and `GET /dps/{id}`, `GET /nfse/{chave}`, the cancellation `e101101` and
12//!   the agreement of the city;
13//! - **recovery after a lost answer**, by the same DPS Id: the part that matters most at a counter.
14//!
15//! Everything leaves through [`ravenrs_dfe::Transport`]; [`sandbox::SefinSandbox`] intercepts it
16//! the way MSW does, and checks what the Sefin would check.
17//!
18//! ```compile_fail,E0599
19//! let producao = ravenrs_nfse::Ambiente::Producao;
20//! ```
21//!
22//! ```
23//! use ravenrs_nfse::sandbox::SefinSandbox;
24//! use ravenrs_nfse::{Ambiente, BetaAck, Emissor, exemplo};
25//!
26//! let emissor = Emissor::builder(BetaAck::i_understand_this_is_experimental())
27//!     .ambiente(Ambiente::Sandbox)
28//!     .signer(ravenrs_dfe::certificados::certificado_curinga()?)
29//!     .transport(SefinSandbox::new())
30//!     .build()?;
31//! let emitida = emissor.emitir(&exemplo::dps(1))?;
32//! assert_eq!(emitida.chave.as_str().len(), 50);
33//! # Ok::<(), Box<dyn std::error::Error>>(())
34//! ```

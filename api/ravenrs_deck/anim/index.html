Source
Expand description

Slide show timing for DeckCraft.

  • Timeline: a slide’s animation list grouped into click steps, with per-shape (and per-paragraph) AnimState at any point of the show.
  • transition_layers: a slide transition frame as textured quads of the old/new slide images.
  • morph_frame: a Morph transition frame (shapes matched by morph_pairs, moved by morph_xfrm).
  • ShowState: slide show navigation (hidden slides, custom shows, loop, timings), what was played on each visited slide, and what a click does (ShowState::click).

Pure logic: no rendering, no UI toolkit. Every function accepts hostile numbers (NaN, huge or negative times) and never panics.

morph_crop
The crop (left, top, right, bottom) of a picture morphing from box xa cropped by ca to box xb cropped by cb, when its box is x at progress t: the whole image moves and scales linearly between where it lies on either slide, and the crop is what of it x shows. Falls back to blending the crops when a crop leaves nothing visible.
morph_frame
Morph frame from slide a to slide b at progress t (0..1, eased here). Paired shapes (morph_pairs) move along morph_xfrm; when they differ only in solid fill colour the colour blends, when pictures differ only in crop the crop morphs (morph_crop), when only in geometry (and fill colour) the outline morphs (MorphFrame::paths), otherwise the new shape fades in under the fading old one on the same moving box; with the transition option words or characters their texts are listed in MorphFrame::text to morph word by word or character by character. Unpaired shapes of a fade out, unpaired shapes of b fade in, and shapes of b for which hidden_b is true (entrance animations, hidden media) stay out of the frame.
morph_pairs
Pairs of (shape on a, shape on b) that Morph animates from one to the other: first by !!name (forced match), then by exact name, then by the same kind and text, then by the same kind and preset geometry if neither carries text. A text box with neither a name nor its text on the other slide stays unpaired and fades in place, as in PowerPoint (#119). Each shape is used once; top-level shapes only (groups move whole).
morph_xfrm
Interpolate a box from a to b at t (0..1): position and size linearly, rotation along the shortest way round; flips switch at the midpoint.
show_order
Slide indices the show visits in order: the custom show or range from Set Up Slide Show, minus hidden slides.
transition_frame
Layers of the transition kind with option at linear progress t (0..1; the effect applies its own easing) on a slide aspect wide per unit of height. Draw in order over black. At t >= 1 the result is the new slide alone; at t <= 0 (or NaN) the old slide alone. Morph returns a plain crossfade (the UI renders morph frames with crate::anim::morph_frame). Layers with Source::is_part sources need the slides rendered as background and content (uses_slide_parts).
transition_layers
Layers of the transition kind (a crate::model::anim::TRANSITIONS id) with option at progress t (0..1) on a 16:9 slide. See transition_frame.
uses_slide_parts
Does kind animate the slides’ backgrounds and contents separately (PowerPoint’s Dynamic Content transitions and Reveal)? Then the frame uses Source::OldBack & co.

In-place text editing: entering/leaving text, typing, deleting, caret movement and selection.

Structs§

AutoList
A list AutoFormat started: the text being edited right after it, the typed lead with its space, the paragraph’s own formatting before, and the revision the change left.
ImeSurrounding
Bounded committed text around an IME selection, in logical (not visual) order and with UTF-8 byte offsets. The current input-method composition (pre-edit) is removed from both the text and the offsets.

Functions§

body_of
The text body a text selection edits.
caret_color
The colour of text typed at the caret. The canvas draws the caret in it, so the caret shows wherever the text does: light on a dark slide, dark on a light one. None when that text has no fill to take a colour from.
caret_line
The insertion point on the slide: its line from top to bottom, in slide coordinates (flips and vertical text as drawn). Text with no paragraph yet (a placeholder about to be typed in) is measured as one empty paragraph, so the caret shows where typing begins. None for notes, which are an egui text field.
cell_range_underlines
Like range_underlines, for the text of table id’s cell at row, col.
composition_underlines
Underlines under an input method’s composition (#101), in slide coordinates: (from, to, thick) per line, thick under the part being converted. Empty without a composition, or for notes (an egui text field draws its own composition).
correct_word
What a just-typed word becomes (None: unchanged). sentence_start: nothing but spaces or a sentence’s end (., !, ?) before it in the paragraph. typing: one character was typed (the case rules apply only then).
fit_text_box
Refit a shape after its text changed: remember the shrink-on-overflow scale the layout computes (#724), then grow/shrink an auto-sizing text box (Resize shape to fit text) to its text.
format_range
What format_run formats while editing: the selection, or for a bare caret the word it’s in (without its trailing space). Empty when the caret is at a word boundary. Insert Hyperlink edits this same span, so the dialog reads it too.
ime_surrounding
Committed surrounding text for the text being edited.
layout_for
Lay out the text being edited (for vertical caret movement and hit testing).
range_underlines
Underlines (slide coordinates) under character ranges (para, start, end) of shape id’s text as laid out on the current slide: where spelling squiggles go.
specs
Catalogue text-editing, selection, autocorrect, font, IME, and surrounding-deletion commands with their parameter docs and enabled predicates.
text_frame
The text being edited as it is drawn: its layout, and the transform from layout space to the slide (flips don’t mirror text, vertical text is turned). Shapes and table cells alike; the caret, the text selection, hit testing and caret movement all use it. None for notes.
text_pos_at
The text position under slide point p in the text being edited (its shape or cell).

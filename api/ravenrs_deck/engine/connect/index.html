Module connect 

Source
Expand description

Connectors glued to shapes: connection sites, snapping line ends to them, and rerouting attached connectors whenever the shapes they connect move, resize or rotate.

Functions§

endpoints
Line endpoints (start, end) of a line-like shape’s box.
locate
The shape id anywhere in shapes (a group’s member included) with the affine that maps the space its box lives in onto the slide: a connector glues to a grouped shape, as in PowerPoint, and its sites are where the group draws them.
nearest_site
The nearest connection site within radius of p on any visible shape except exclude.
reroute
Move the ends of connectors glued to shapes on the edited slide to the current site positions. Connections to shapes that no longer exist are dropped.
set_glue
Glue end end (0 = start, 1 = end) of line id to to (or unglue with None). Plain lines become connectors when glued.
site_shape_at
The shape whose sites should show while drawing or dragging a connector end near p.
sites
Connection sites of a top-level shape s in slide coordinates (index = site index stored on connectors). For a shape inside a group use sites_of or sites_in.
sites_in
Connection sites of s in slide coordinates, to_abs mapping the space its box lives in (the slide, or the child space of the groups around it) onto the slide.
sites_of
Connection sites of shape id anywhere in shapes, in slide coordinates; None when there is no such shape.

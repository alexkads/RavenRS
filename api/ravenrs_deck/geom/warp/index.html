WordArt text warps (DrawingML a:bodyPr/a:prstTxWarp).

A warp preset is a list of bands laid over the shape’s text rectangle (insets are ignored). Text lines are shared out over the bands in order (the first ⌈n/k⌉ lines to the first band and so on). A band either

  • stretches its lines between a top and a bottom guide (Band::Fill): the ink box of the band’s lines fills the band vertically (a band whose ink is shorter than the tallest band’s keeps the same scale, centred), and horizontally the text is scaled so that the widest band is filled along the mean length of its guides (one scale for the whole text), or
  • sets its lines at their natural size along one guide (Band::Follow, arches and circles): glyphs keep their size, are placed by arc length (aligned as in the paragraph, the whole guide being the line’s width: insets don’t count) and stand on the guide’s left normal; lines further from the guide stack outwards. Lines that are longer than the guide are shrunk to fit.

Positions along a guide are by arc length; a fill band maps the same fraction of its top and bottom guide’s length and interpolates between them. The guide curves were derived from observation: synthetic decks of I bars were drawn with PowerPoint and every curve fitted to the measured bar ends (most fit within a point on a 420 × 200 pt box).

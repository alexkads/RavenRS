pub fn detach_placeholder(ctx: &Ctx<'_>, shape: &mut Shape)
Expand description

Turn a placeholder into a regular shape that looks the same: what it inherited from its layout and master placeholders (position, fill, line, effects, style, body properties and the text style of every outline level, the master’s title or body style included) becomes its own, and ph is cleared. A shape that is not a placeholder is left as it is.

Theme references (+mj-lt, scheme colours) stay references, so the copy still follows the destination theme, as PowerPoint’s default paste does.

pub fn crop_window(crop: [f32; 4]) -> [f64; 4]
Expand description

The part of a picture’s source that crop (left, top, right, bottom fractions of the source) keeps, as fractions of the source: [left, top, width, height], bitmap or metafile alike. Each side is taken as 0–1 (junk as 0). A negative side pads the source instead of cutting it (down to -100), as a negative a:srcRect side does in Office files and a picture fitted whole into a frame of another shape needs: the window then starts before the source or ends past it. When the two sides of an axis leave less than 0.5% of it, the left (top) side stands and 0.5% shows.

Module write 

Source
Expand description

EMF and WMF writers (feature write).

The writers take a list of drawing operations (Op) in paint order, in points with y down, and the region of that space the picture covers: its top-left corner is the picture’s origin and its size the picture’s size. What an application’s document holds beyond these operations (gradients, patterns, type, effects, transparency of paths) is turned into them by the application.

  • EMF (emf): paths keep their Bézier curves; fills are solid brushes; strokes are geometric pens with their width, cap, join, miter limit and dashes; clips are path clips inside a saved device context; images keep their pixels and their transparency and may be turned or skewed. The header’s frame is the region in 0.01 mm.
  • WMF (wmf): the same operations with curves flattened into polygons and 16-bit coordinates, behind a placeable header that gives the size. WMF has no clip paths, no transparency and no turned images; Wmf says which of these the picture lost.

The reader reads back what the writers write: the tests round-trip both formats through crate::craft::metafile::parse.

Chapter lists, in the two forms players read.

  • QuickTime chapter track (QuickTime File Format, “Chapter lists” and “Text media”): a disabled text track whose samples are the chapter titles, referenced from a video or audio track by a tref/chap track reference. QuickTime Player, Apple TV, iOS and Final Cut read this form. Each sample is a 16-bit big-endian length, the title, and an encd atom with value 0x100 marking the text as UTF-8 (the convention Apple’s own chapter tracks in .m4a/.m4v files use; plain QuickTime text is Mac Roman).
  • Nero chapter list moov/udta/chpl, version 1: u32 reserved, u8 chapter count, then per chapter a u64 start in 100 ns units, a u8 title length and the UTF-8 title. It has no official specification; this is the layout publicly documented for Nero Digital files and written by many tools. VLC and ffprobe (-show_chapters) read it.

Both forms carry at most MAX_CHAPTERS chapters with titles of at most MAX_CHAPTER_TITLE_BYTES bytes (the chpl count and length fields are one byte each).

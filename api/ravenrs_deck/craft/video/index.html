Module video 

Source
Expand description

Clean-room pure-Rust video codecs for the Craft apps, one feature per codec. Input and output are byte slices and planar sample buffers (no filesystem access of its own); builds for wasm32-unknown-unknown.

FeatureModuleCodec
dnx[dnx]Avid DNxHD / DNxHR (SMPTE ST 2019-1): decoder and encoder
apv[apv]APV (RFC 9924): decoder (apv-decode) and encoder (apv-encode)
vp8[vp8]VP8 (RFC 6386): decoder (vp8-decode) and key-frame encoder (vp8-encode)
mpeg2[mpeg2]MPEG-2 video (ITU-T H.262) and MPEG-1 video: decoder
mpeg4[mpeg4]MPEG-4 Part 2 video (ISO/IEC 14496-2, Xvid/DivX): Simple profile encoder (mpeg4-encode)
prores[prores]Apple ProRes (SMPTE RDD 36): decoder and encoder
h264h264ITU-T H.264 / AVC: decoder (h264-decode) and encoder (h264-encode)
hevchevcITU-T H.265 / HEVC: decoder (hevc-decode) and encoder (hevc-encode)
vp9vp9VP9: decoder (vp9-decode) and encoder (vp9-encode)
av1av1AV1 (AOMedia Video 1): decoder (av1-decode) and encoder (av1-encode)

No codec is on by default. Feature threads spreads the work of the codecs that support it over rayon worker threads.

The decoders bound the pictures they hold with a memory budget (budget).

Containers and bit-level I/O are in craft-media; the codecs here share its bitstream module and describe their streams with crate::craft::media::CodecParams.

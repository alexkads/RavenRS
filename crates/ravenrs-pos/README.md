# ravenrs-pos

The rules of a point of sale for [RavenRS](https://github.com/alexkads/RavenRS), with nothing about screens or fiscal documents: a cart, what a scanner reads (`3*7891000100103`), totals in exact decimals, payments and change. Any front, in a window, in the browser or in a test, works on the same rules.

`ravenrs-app-pos` puts screens and records on it.
